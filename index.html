About

From experimental energy conversion to dynamic energy decisions.

My research path began with hands-on experimental work on hydrogen production from biogas and later expanded into process simulation, data-driven modeling, and optimization. At Columbia, I am developing dynamic models and economic MPC frameworks for integrated CO2-to-ethanol systems under variable renewable power.

I am especially interested in how learning-based methods can accelerate optimization and control while respecting physical relationships and strict operational constraints.
