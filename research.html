Research

Research

My work connects physical modeling, optimization, machine learning, and control for sustainable energy conversion and low-carbon fuel production.

Current

Dynamic optimization and control of integrated CO2-to-ethanol systems

I am developing an integrated Simulink model coupling PEM water electrolysis, H2 storage, and downstream CO2-to-ethanol production under variable renewable power, together with an economic MPC framework for operational decision-making.

Learning + Control

XGBoost surrogate modeling for nonlinear dynamic systems

I am building surrogate models from Simulink-generated data to improve the computational efficiency of repeated prediction and control optimization while preserving the feasibility of operational decisions.

Systems

Dynamic techno-economic and siting analysis

I evaluate levelized production costs across regional differences in renewable resources, CO2 availability, water access, storage conditions, and system operation.

Earlier Work

Experimental, data-driven, and process-level foundations.

China University of Petroleum-Beijing · 2024-2025

AutoML-based optimization of biogas-to-syngas conversion

Simulated biogas-to-syngas operating cases in Aspen Plus, curated validated simulation datasets, and coupled AutoGluon surrogate modeling with multi-objective particle swarm optimization and techno-economic analysis.

China University of Petroleum-Beijing · 2023-2024

Hydrogen production from biogas reforming

Operated a HY-B experimental unit during a 42-day campaign and developed a data-driven workflow using AutoGluon, SHAP, and MOPSO to identify operating regimes that improved hydrogen yield.

China University of Petroleum-Beijing · 2023-present

Modified biochar for anaerobic digestion systems

Supported modified-biochar preparation, material characterization, and microbial/metagenomic analysis for anaerobic digestion systems and antibiotic resistance gene removal.

Earlier projects

Organic waste treatment and gravity energy storage

Worked on machine-learning analysis of anaerobic digestion data, sodium-ion battery material ideas from biomass derivatives, and a laboratory gravity-energy-storage device project.
